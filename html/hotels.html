The Connaught

The Connaught is a hotel smack dab in the middle of London. Perfect for anyone who wants to explore London, and it also has a directly connected restaurant for convenience!
Its address is Glouster Place, Carlos Pl, London W1K 2AL. The hotel is known for excellent service and has a high average rating of 4.4 out of 5!

It is located at Amesbury, Salisbury SP4 7AS.
