England is a very influential country, in large part due to colonization efforts that they've made in the past. This is why English is used in countries so far away from England, for example.
The national flower of England is the Tudor rose. It represents the union of York and the Houses of Lancaster, which was the reason England was founded.

England is part of the United Kingdom, which used to be the largest monarchy ever in terms of land area due to the various colonies it possessed. However, now that most of them have claimed independence, the UK remains as but a fraction of its former size.
